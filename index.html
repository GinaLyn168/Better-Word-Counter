Privacy Policy for Better Word Counter

Effective Date: August 31, 2026

Overview

Better Word Counter is a Google Docs™ add-on designed to calculate word counts and manage excluded phrases.

Data Access and Storage

  • The add-on only accesses text in the active document via documents.currentonly to calculate word totals and detect exclusion phrases. This text is processed entirely in memory in your session and is never transmitted to external servers or shared with third parties.
  • Saved exclusion phrases are stored exclusively inside the active Google Doc's internal metadata using Google Apps Script's DocumentProperties. We do not maintain any external database.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, or telemetry data.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data resides strictly inside your Google Doc, deleting the exclusion list inside the add-on or deleting the document itself permanently deletes all associated data.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the project repository or contact the developer via GitHub.
